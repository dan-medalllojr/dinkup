# Change 10: The last unpinned court (Pino → ML Lifestyle Park)

**Date:** 2026-10-02 (after launch)

## Prompt

> go with 4-8 improvements for now

Item 5 was "Pino Pickleball Court is still off the map". In [change 3](change-3-court-pins.md) it was left unpinned, because Google had renamed the spot "ML Lifestyle Park - Sports & Food Park" and nobody could say whether the courts were still there.

## What the research found

A research agent looked for evidence that the courts are operating now. The main AI then re-checked the key evidence itself:

- **The operator's booking site** (`webpos.lavielifestyle.ph`, "Book a Court · ML Lifestyle Park") lists **Pickleball Courts 1–9**, plus basketball and futsal, with live bookings this week. The payment biller is still named "LA VIE - PINO TALAMBAN". So it's a rename, not a closure.
- **Reclub meets** for "Pino Pickleball Courts" in 2026 link a map point of exactly **10.376937, 123.91863**, with a place ID that decodes to the same Google place the directories embed.
- **Nominatim** puts that point on **San Jose Road**, as the directories said.
- PickleBoard's separate "ML Lifestyle Park" listing is a duplicate of the same venue (same phone, website, and reviews). **Its pin is wrong**: about 300 m away, on a residential street in Tigbao, probably geocoded from an address. It wasn't used.
- cebupickleballcourts.com's "6 courts" is out of date; the operator's own site shows 9.

## Result

Seeded as **"ML Lifestyle Park (Pino Pickleball)"**, so players searching for either name find it (change 4's search matches every word). It's listed with 9 outdoor courts and a note that it also has basketball and futsal, with booking at the operator's site. **Every venue from the original list is now on the map: 22 courts.**
